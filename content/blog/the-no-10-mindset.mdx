A No. 10 doesn't just receive the ball. They arrive with a plan.

The space between a defensive midfielder and the centre-back line — the half-spaces, the seams, the pockets — is where games are decided. The job isn't to find the ball. The job is to be **already turned**, already scanning, already a half-yard ahead of the player marking you.

## Three habits that change the position

1. **Scan before the pass arrives.** Two looks: one over the shoulder before the ball leaves the passer, one as the ball is travelling.
2. **Receive on the back foot.** Your first touch should take you out of pressure, not into it.
3. **Play the obvious pass when the brave one isn't on.** The brave pass is only brave if it's the right pass.

## Why it matters

Modern football compresses space. The teams that win move the ball through the lines under pressure — and that's the No. 10's job. Carry the team's tempo. Set the rhythm. And when the moment arrives, decide.

> Vision is a habit before it's a talent.

That's the mindset. Now train it.

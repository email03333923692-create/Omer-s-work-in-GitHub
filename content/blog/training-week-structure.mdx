Talent gets you noticed. Structure gets you signed.

Here's how I break a typical week:

## Monday — Recovery + Mobility
Active recovery. Mobility flow, light cardio, sauna or contrast bath if available. The goal is to come back to Tuesday feeling fresh, not heroic.

## Tuesday — Strength + Tactical
Lower-body strength block (squat, RDL, single-leg work) followed by a tactical session: build-up patterns, third-man combinations, finishing in tight spaces.

## Wednesday — High-Intensity Football
Full football session. Small-sided games (4v4, 6v6+2). High tempo, lots of reps, lots of decisions per minute.

## Thursday — Upper + Skills
Upper-body strength + ball mastery work. Wall passes, first-touch reps under pressure, weak-foot drills.

## Friday — Pre-Match Activation
Light, sharp, technical. Set pieces. Patterns. Walk through the game plan.

## Saturday — Match Day
Compete. Execute. Leave it on the pitch.

## Sunday — Full Recovery
Sleep, food, family. Mind off football.

> The best players I've watched aren't the ones who train hardest. They're the ones who train *smartest* — and then sleep on it.
